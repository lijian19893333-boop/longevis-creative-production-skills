# Requirements: source, date and scope

Checked **2026-10-22** against the National Copyright Administration's [Computer Software Copyright Registration Measures, official PDF](https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/flfg/bmgz/202410/P020241015604759788122.pdf), particularly Articles 9–12 and 17. Ordinary identification materials use consecutive first/last 30 pages of source and one documentation type; material shorter than 60 pages is supplied in full. Outside special cases, the rule specifies at least 50 lines per program page and 30 per documentation page. Article 12 provides alternative deposit arrangements; do not improvise redaction rules.

The rule does not establish a universal “500 Chinese characters for main functions” requirement. That target must come from the current drafter or form.

For every actual application, reopen current official guidance and inspect the filing portal's applicable instructions. Record the URL, access date, article or field, current agency request and how the case was handled. This cached reference does not establish current upload limits, forms, online procedures, fees, processing times or acceptance of a particular layout. Resolve special short-code, graphical-platform and confidentiality cases with the current official guidance/drafter while continuing independent preparation.

## Avoid converting assumptions into requirements

| Item | Treatment |
|---|---|
| 50 nonempty original lines in the helper | Conservative local layout/counting choice; inspect actual rendered pages |
| 500-character narrative | Current agency/project target when requested |
| Both operation and design documents | Provide when requested/useful; do not claim universal necessity |
| APK or real-device capture | Useful version/runtime evidence; absence alone is not asserted here to be a statutory rejection rule |
| Native HMI project plus scripts | Scope/export review needed; file presence alone is not complete-program proof |
| Reasonable proposed dates | Mark as proposals; obtain factual confirmation before final declarations |

This skill prepares materials. It does not determine disputed ownership or guarantee registration.
