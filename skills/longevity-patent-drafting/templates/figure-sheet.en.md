Leave unknown coordinates blank. Identify recognizable surfaces or boundaries for parts, holes, grooves and channels. Similar parts do not share identity. Image coordinates are not engineering dimensions.

Inspect every number, leader, endpoint, component register and text reference. Save context screenshots for small targets where useful. Model, camera, explosion or page changes invalidate affected reviews. Keep text independently editable and distinguish leaders from motion, flow and signal lines.

- Representative figure checks: evidence, proportions, occlusion, orientation, line quality and readability at final size.
- Outputs: model/2D source, unlabeled figure, annotated figure, print/bitmap export; record version, purpose, observed verification and status for each.
- Plans and sketches are not completed formal figures.
