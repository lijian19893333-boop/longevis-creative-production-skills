# Common Failures and Remedies

| Recurring failure | Effective alternative |
|---|---|
| Build boxes from a front-view impression and patch repeatedly | Establish shared front/side/top contours and cross-sections; unify clear space, wall thickness, and front-door relationships |
| Place a new shell over the old complete product | Define an explicit CAD reuse allowlist and exclude the old front compartment, hidden shell, and duplicate chassis |
| Fill gaps with thick walls until the interior shrinks | Define inner/outer contours separately; share boundaries between door, sidewall, and floor |
| Interpret a protruding panel as lateral curvature | Read the side profile; keep the display flat and derive protrusion from depth and tilt |
| Deliver after the front looks similar and ask the user to discover errors | Inspect every view independently and provide corresponding evidence; recheck affected views together |
| Enlarge a thumbnail and call approximate colors official UI | Extract the original/embedded image, rectify perspective, preserve colors, and inspect the texture and display plane |
| Mirror geometry based only on image-left/image-right | Identify layouts from the relative directions of viewing face, door face, and operating face |
| Replace opening motion with transparent walls | Rotate a real door group and keep the fixed enclosure; provide separate disassembly functionality |
| Make one button open every door | Support independent door states and verify the other door does not reset |
| Claim a side-panel preview opened when only a tool screenshot or queue exists | Verify the server, page load, and user-accessible entry point; disclose queued opening |
| Treat every small modeling fragment as an engineering component | Organize by assembly meaning and separate render assets from solid engineering CAD |
| Guess hidden mechanisms or iterate endlessly for “1:1” | Converge on evidence-backed geometry, label unobserved areas fitted, and do not invent engineering precision |

The method grew from modeling curved open enclosures and closed rectangular equipment. It does not inherit their heights, cross-sections, branding, display dimensions, opening angles, or colors. Establish constraints anew for each product. Dimensional accuracy and projected resemblance require separate checks.
